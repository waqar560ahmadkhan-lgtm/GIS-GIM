ℹ️ About Waqar GIS Portal

Waqar GIS Portal is a high-performance web-based spatial analysis and mapping application engineered in accordance with modern Geomatics and spatial software standards.

The system seamlessly integrates multi-source high-resolution satellite imagery (Google, Sentinel-2, Esri) with interactive vector digitization tools, facilitating land area calculations, spatial buffering, and topology validation.

🛠️ Services & Spatial Solutions

The portal provides the following technical solutions and services:

  • Spatial Digitization: Digitizing polygons, lines, and point features with automated perimeter and area measurements.
  • Massavi Conversion: Automatic calculation of square meters into traditional land units (Kanal, Marla, Square Feet).
  • Buffer Analysis: Generating custom proximity buffer zones around features.
  • Multi-Source Basemaps: Seamless basemap switching between Google Satellite, Sentinel-2, OpenStreetMap, and Dark Canvas.
  • Professional Exports: Downloading vector layers and tables as GeoJSON, CSV, high-resolution PNG images, and PDF reports.

❓ Help & FAQ

Q1: How do I digitize a new feature?
A: Click the polygon or line tool on the top-left Leaflet Draw toolbar and place vertices directly on the map.

Q2: Where are the land area conversions displayed?
A: Once a feature is drawn, the area automatically calculates in Kanal, Marla, and Square Feet inside the bottom Attribute Table.
